Before you arriveSend your passport page
TM30 registration

Thai law requires hosts to register foreign guests' address with Immigration (form TM30). Please send a photo of your passport page to Sira in the Airbnb chat so it can be filed for you.

Living at Knightsbridge Prime Sathon

The building

To reach residents-only areas and the rooftop, register with your passport, a face scan and a fingerprint. The condo staff will help you with this in the lobby.

Your keycard is needed for every elevator ride, up and down. The gym and pool also need a fingerprint scan.
